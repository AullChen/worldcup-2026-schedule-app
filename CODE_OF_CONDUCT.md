# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to make this project a respectful and productive space for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification when needed.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participants who do not follow this code of conduct.
